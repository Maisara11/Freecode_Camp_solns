Donuts recipe

Here is a classic recipe for soft, fluffy homemade glazed donuts.

Ingredients

  • Flour(3 and 1/4 cups)
  • Milk(3/4 cups)
  • Yeast(2 and 1/4 cups)
  • Sugar(1/3 cup)
  • Butter(1/4 cup)
  • Eggs(2 large)
  • Vanilla(1 tsp)
  • Salt(1/2 tsp)
  • Oil
  • Powdered sugar(2 cups)
  • Milk or Heavy cream(1/3 cup)

    • Instructions

    1. Mix warm milk,sugar,and yeast in a bowl and let it sit for 5-10 minutes until frothy.
    2. Add eggs,melted butter,Vanilla and salt to the yeast mixture.
    3. Stir in flour gradually until a dough forms
    4. Knead for 5-7 minutes until smooth.
    5. Put the dough in a greased bowl, coover it and let it rise in a warm place for 1.5 to 2 hours(until doubled in size)
    6. Roll out the dough to 1/2-inch thickness on afloured surface.
    7. Cut into donut shapes using a donut cutter or glass.
    8. Place on a basking sheet, cover and let rise for 30-45 minutes until puffy.
    9. Heat 2 inches of oil in apot to 350 degrees fahrenheit(175 degrees Celcius).
    10. Fry donuts for 1 minute per side until golden brown.
    11. Drain on paper towels.
    12. Whisk powdered sugar,milk,and vanilla in a small bowl until smooth
    13. Dip warm donuts into the glaze and set on a wire rack.
